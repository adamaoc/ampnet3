## Primary pitch (~30 seconds)

**AmpNet Media** is a creative media studio where video, code, campaigns, and social come together in one place. We blend creativity with technology to build digital experiences that flow, inspire, and connect—not just sites and ads that “work,” but work people remember. From cinematic web video and strategic web advertising to responsive development and social that feels human, we’re the team you call when you want the full stack of modern digital storytelling, backed by transparent pricing and the option to run on your infrastructure or ours.

## One-liner (~10 seconds)

We’re **ampnet (media)**—a creative studio that crafts **digital magic**: video, web, ads, and social, built to move people and grow your business.

---

## Extended pitch (~45–60 seconds)

AmpNet Media sits at the intersection of **creativity and technology**. We’re developers, storytellers, and strategists who treat every project like a canvas—whether that’s a launch site, a corporate presence, a campaign, or content for social.

Our **four pillars** are **web video** (cinematic storytelling that converts), **web development** (fast, fluid, future-ready experiences), **web advertising** (campaigns that resonate and reach), and **social media** (authentic connection in crowded feeds). On [ampnet.media](https://ampnet.media) you can browse **our work** and **live website builds**, see **how we price** projects—from marketing pages to full apps—and **start a conversation** when you’re ready. We also build toward a connected ecosystem (including **FlexHub**) so content and sites can scale with you over time.

**Footer line we use:** *Crafting digital experiences with love.*

---

## What the site backs up (proof points)

- Portfolio: **Works** gallery and **Websites** showcase (fed by FlexHub at build time).
- **Services** section: the four pillars above, with clear positioning.
- **Pricing**: engagement models (client-owned vs. developer-owned infra) and project tiers from static pages through SaaS-scale builds.
- **About** + team story; **Contact** for new work.
- **FlexHub** ([flexhub.ampnet.media](https://flexhub.ampnet.media)): multi-site CMS, content, and APIs that support how we ship and manage client properties.

Use the **primary** pitch for most conversations; use the **one-liner** for intros and bios; use the **extended** version for investors, partners, or anyone who wants the full picture in under a minute.
